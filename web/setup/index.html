Microphone

Pick the mic you talk into. A dedicated input (not "Stereo Mix" or desktop audio) keeps game sound out.

Processor

Where the speech and emotion models run. A GPU makes each sentence much faster.

More about processors

NVIDIA cards, AMD cards with ROCm and Apple Silicon Macs all work as a GPU. Switching reloads the models in the background; the overlay keeps working meanwhile.

Images

Add images or GIFs for each emotion: click Add, paste a Link, or drop files on a box.

More about images
  • Tenor and Giphy page links work, and you can drag a GIF straight from another browser tab.
  • Click ★ to make an image the default: it's shown for any emotion with no images of its own.
  • The dropdown under each image sets when it's used. Mild only: when the app is fairly sure. Strong only: when it's very sure (Settings → Strong images from). Always: both.
  • Each GIF starts from its first frame whenever it appears.
  • Images for word triggers are in Word triggers.

Add the overlay to OBS

  1. In OBS, add a Browser source.
  2. Paste this address as its URL, and set the width and height to your image size (e.g. 800 × 800).
  3. Leave custom CSS empty: the background is already transparent.
Open

URL options

Add ?fit=cover to fill the source and crop, or ?label=1 to show the emotion name (handy for testing).

Switch scenes and filters

Let each emotion switch an OBS scene or turn on a filter.

How to connect

In OBS 28 or newer: Tools → WebSocket Server Settings, tick Enable WebSocket server, and use Show Connect Info for the port and password. These settings stay on this PC and aren't included in backups.

Word triggers

Show a special image for a few seconds when you say a word or phrase, like "gg" or "let's go".

How matching works
  • Capitals don't matter, and only whole words count, so "gg" won't fire on "eggs".
  • Separate alternatives with commas: gg, good game.
  • If a word doesn't fire, check how it was heard under Last heard on the Live page.

Hotkeys

Keys that work in any app, even while a game has focus. Click a key combination to change it.

More about hotkeys
  • Each one can hold an emotion, show any image (word-trigger images too), fire a word trigger, or go back to automatic.
  • A held emotion or image stays until you press its keys again or a "Back to automatic" key.
  • Keys need Ctrl, Alt or Win so they don't block typing. F1–F24, Pause and Scroll Lock also work on their own, which suits a Stream Deck or macro pad.
  • Windows only. Hotkeys stay on this PC and aren't included in backups.

Calibration

Read 14 short lines, each in the emotion shown (about two minutes). The app learns how your voice and mic sound and recommends settings; you choose which to apply.

More about calibration

Say each line naturally and pause after it; the next line appears by itself. It tunes the app to your pauses, your volume, how expressive you are, and whether tone of voice helps for you. Only numbers are kept, never your audio or words.

Calibrate from a long recording

People sound different while playing than when reading lines. This calibrates on your real speech: record yourself while you play, or use a past stream.

How it works

The app finds every sentence, fixes obvious skews by itself (like the tone-of-voice model hearing calm game talk as "sad"), then lets you check its guesses. The more sentences you check, the better it fits you. Your audio and words stay in memory only and are gone when you finish.

Changes apply immediately and are saved to config.yaml.
