Images

Add images per emotion: click "Add" or drop files on a box. GIFs work too, from a file, or from a link with "Link" (Tenor and Giphy page links included); you can also drag a GIF straight from another browser tab. Each GIF starts from its first frame whenever it appears. Click ★ to mark the default image, which is shown for any emotion with no images. The dropdown under each image sets its intensity: Mild and Strong images are picked by how confident the detection is, and Any works for both.

Word triggers

Show a special image for a few seconds when you say a word or phrase, like "gg" or "let's go". Matching ignores capitals and only counts whole words, so "gg" won't fire on "eggs". Separate alternatives with commas. If a word doesn't fire, check how it's spelled in the Live panel's transcript. Put trigger images in Word triggers only above so they never appear for emotions.

Hotkeys

Keys that work in any app, even while a game has focus. Click a key combination to change it, then press the new keys. Each one can hold an emotion, show any image (including your word-trigger images), fire a word trigger, or go back to automatic. A held emotion or image stays until you press its keys again or a "Back to automatic" key. Keys need Ctrl, Alt or Win so they don't block typing; F1–F24, Pause and Scroll Lock also work on their own, which suits a Stream Deck or macro pad. Windows only. These stay on this PC and aren't included in backups.

Settings

Changes apply immediately, with no restart, and are saved to config.yaml.

Calibration

Read 14 short lines, each in the emotion shown. The app learns how your voice and mic sound, then recommends settings tuned to how you speak: your pauses, your volume, how expressive you are, and whether tone of voice helps for you. You choose which to apply. It takes about two minutes. Say each line naturally and pause after it; the next line appears by itself. Only numbers are kept, never your audio or words.

Calibrate from a long recording

People sound different while actually playing than when reading lines: quieter, flatter, faster. This calibrates on your real speech instead. Record yourself while you play, or use a video or audio file of a past stream. The app finds every sentence, fixes obvious skews by itself (like the tone-of-voice model hearing calm game talk as "sad"), and then lets you check its guesses. The more sentences you check, the better it fits you. Your audio and words stay in memory only and are gone when you finish.

OBS scenes and filters

Let the emotion switch OBS scenes or turn filters on and off. In OBS 28 or newer: Tools → WebSocket Server Settings, tick Enable WebSocket server, and use Show Connect Info for the port and password. These settings stay on this PC and aren't included in backups.
