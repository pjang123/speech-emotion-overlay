Images

Add images per emotion (click "Add" or drop files on a box). Click ★ to mark the default image, which is shown for any emotion with no images. The dropdown under each image sets its intensity: Mild and Strong images are picked by how confident the detection is, and Any works for both.

Settings

Changes apply immediately, with no restart, and are saved to config.yaml.

Calibration

Read 14 short lines, each in the emotion shown. The app learns how your voice and mic sound, then recommends settings tuned to how you speak: your pauses, your volume, how expressive you are, and whether tone of voice helps for you. You choose which to apply. It takes about two minutes. Say each line naturally and pause after it; the next line appears by itself. Only numbers are kept, never your audio or words.

Backup

Save all your images and settings as one zip, or restore one. Importing replaces everything currently set up.
